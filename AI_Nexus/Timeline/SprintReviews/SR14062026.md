* *Details:* Identified PyInstaller report artifacts and the Unreal WebBrowser component's CEF Chromium cache as the primary culprits. Pruned and implemented strict gitignores to ensure repository portability and fast transfer speeds.

### 🛡️ 5. Non-Destructive Telemetry Classification (AAS v1.3.4) (Complexity: 5)
* *Achievement:* Resolved initialization race conditions during sequential actor spawning.
* *Details:* Reclassified routine telemetry requests (`push_telemetry`) as non-destructive tools, lowering their AAS validation threshold to `0.4` (meeting the `Unreal_Simulation` baseline VSS of `0.44`). This prevents routine initialization telemetry from consuming the single-use `HANDSHAKE_ACTIVE` global authority boost token.

### 🤝 6. Blueprint Handshake Support (Complexity: 3)
* *Achievement:* Added a dynamic authority-boosting mechanism for real-time play sessions.
