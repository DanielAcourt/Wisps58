# AI_Nexus/Research: The Knowledge Foundation

## 🔬 Purpose
This directory serves as the repository for formal research, academic papers, mathematical proofs, and foundational definitions. It is the "Deep Memory" of the Sovereign Framework, providing the theoretical underpinnings for our cyber-physical systems.

## 📂 Structure

### [Academic/](./Academic/)
Stores whitepapers, research articles, and formal documentation related to robotics, spatial computing, and marine engineering.

### [Mathematics/](./Mathematics/)
Contains mathematical models, algorithms (e.g., PSTA method), and proofs that drive our simulation and decision-making logic.

### [Definitions/](./Definitions/)
The Single Source of Truth (SSoT) for technical and thematic terminology. This section is used to train agents and maintain linguistic consistency across the community and codebase.

## 🤝 Community & Training
This folder is designed to be a living library. Contributions here ensure that both human developers and AI agents share a unified understanding of the complex systems we build.
