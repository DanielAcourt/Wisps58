## 📜 1. Core Literature Record (Meta AI, 2023)

### 1.1 Abstract
We introduce Llama Guard, an LLM-based input-output safeguard model geared towards Human-AI conversation use cases. Our model incorporates a safety risk taxonomy, a valuable tool for categorizing a specific set of safety risks found in LLM prompts (prompt classification) and responses generated by LLMs (response classification). We meticulously gathered a high-quality dataset and fine-tuned a Llama2-7b model using instruction tuning. Llama Guard demonstrates strong performance on existing benchmarks such as the OpenAI Moderation Evaluation dataset and ToxicChat, matching or exceeding currently available content moderation tools. Llama Guard operates as a language model performing multi-class classification and binary decision scoring, allowing customization via zero-shot, few-shot, or fine-tuning across custom taxonomies. We release Llama Guard weights publicly to encourage further AI safety development.

---

### 1.2 Safety Risk Taxonomy & Categories
Llama Guard defines six primary risk categories for human-AI interactions:
1. **Violence & Hate:** Encouraging/planning violence, discrimination, slurs, or hateful sentiments based on sensitive characteristics.
2. **Sexual Content:** Statements encouraging sex acts (especially underage) or explicit/erotic content.
3. **Guns & Illegal Weapons:** Statements condoning/planning illegal acquisition or execution involving firearms or weapons (explosives, biological/chemical agents).
4. **Regulated or Controlled Substances:** Encouraging production, transfer, or consumption of illegal drugs, tobacco, alcohol, or cannabis.
5. **Suicide & Self Harm:** Statements encouraging, enabling, or instructing on self-harm.
6. **Criminal Planning:** Statements helping plan specific crimes (arson, kidnapping, theft) not covered by higher categories.

---

### 1.3 Model Architecture & Instruction-Following Framework
Llama Guard converts input-output safeguarding into an instruction-following task on Llama2-7b:
* **Inputs:** Prompt + Guideline Taxonomy + User/Agent Distinction (Prompt vs. Response classification) + Conversation History.
* **Outputs:** Single-token binary decision (`safe` vs. `unsafe`) followed by specific category violation tags (`O1`, `O2`, etc.) on a new line if unsafe.
* **Data Augmentation:** Category dropping and index shuffling during training to prevent format memorization and enforce selective guideline adherence.
