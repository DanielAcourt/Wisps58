## 🛡️ Layer 1: Stoicism (The Firewall)
*   **The Core Premise:** The absolute preservation of internal judgment, emotional tranquility, and functional composure regardless of external chaos. One has no control over external events, only over the internal reaction to them.
*   **Historically Trapped In:** Self-help books, ancient texts, modern lifestyle blogs, and detached academic philosophy.
*   **Sovereign Application (The Local Iron Knight & PSTA):**
    *   *Concept:* Stoicism is implemented as the **Internal State Firewall**. Just as a human Stoic filters emotional noise, our system filters toxic, malformed, or unvetted inputs before they reach the core logic.
    *   *Operational Bridge:* It corresponds directly to the **Psychological (P) Pillar** of our PSTA Core. The biometric stress levels of the human operator (e.g., dynamic cognitive load) are isolated so that external operational stress does not degrade system baseline judgments.
    *   *Iron Knight Integration:* Employs robust input-validation gates. When external network telemetry or unvetted API payloads fluctuate erratically, the Iron Knight maintains a steady, deterministic internal processing rate, protecting local execution pipelines from "panic-induced" resource leaks or buffer overflows.
