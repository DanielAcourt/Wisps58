## 2. Technical Milestones: The "Sovereign" Evolution
Our roadmap follows a clear escalation of technical complexity, moving from stationary telemetry to mobile autonomous systems.

### Phase I: The "Sovereign" Telemetry Hub (IoT & Digital Twins)
*   **Focus:** Real-time environmental synchronization (The "Fish Tank" Alpha).
*   **Technical Outcome:** Implementation of deterministic JSON/Binary logging and state management. Establishing the "Sim-to-Real" bridge where physical sensor data (temperature, pH, depth) drives real-time simulation logic in Unreal Engine.

### Phase II: Hardware Sovereignty (The "Black Box" Protocol)
*   **Focus:** Autonomous data survivability (The "Message in a Bottle" System).
*   **Technical Outcome:** Engineering decoupled, low-power logging systems capable of independent operation in extreme environments. This phase focuses on data persistence and hardware-software hardening for critical failure scenarios.

### Phase III: Spatial Intelligence & LiDAR Streaming
*   **Focus:** Runtime reconstruction of scanned environments (The Digital Museum).
*   **Technical Outcome:** Transitioning from static asset rendering to **Dynamic Lidar Stream Processing**. We are architecting systems that ingest high-frequency spatial data for real-time visualization and autonomous navigation, bypassing the constraints of traditional pre-compiled game engine workflows.

## 3. The "Sovereign Soul" Framework
The bedrock of our ecosystem is the **USovereignSaveableEntityComponent**, colloquially known as the **"Sovereign Soul."**

*   **Architectural Significance:** This component acts as the **Single Source of Truth (SSoT)** for an entity's identity, state, and history. It ensures perfect data parity between physical hardware and its digital simulation.
*   **Scalability:** By architecting around this central hub, we ensure all developed logic is modular and class-agnostic, allowing rapid deployment across diverse hardware platforms—from localized sensor arrays to commercial-grade marine vessels.
